Google Ads vs Meta Ads 2026: Delhi Business ke Liye Kaun Better Hai?

Delhi ka har business owner ek hi sawal poochta hai — Google Ads chalau ya Meta (Facebook-Instagram) Ads? 1. Pehle basic samjho: intent vs interruption ka fark

Dono platforms ka sabse bada fark ek line me samjho — Google Ads pakadta hai demand ko, Meta Ads banata hai demand ko. Google par customer khud search karta hai — dentist Rohini, 2BHK Dwarka, lehenga Karol Bagh — matlab woh kharidne ke mood me hai. Ise kehte hain high-intent traffic. Meta (Facebook-Instagram) par customer scroll kar raha hota hai, use kharidne ka iraada nahi hota — tumhara ad use rokta hai aur desire jagata hai. Ise kehte hain interruption ya discovery traffic.

Isi fark se poori strategy banti hai. Google Search Ads me tum keywords par bid karte ho — jo search karega wahi ad dekhega. Meta me tum audience par bid karte ho — age, interest, location aur behaviour ke hisab se ad dikhta hai. Google me customer apni problem bata chuka hai, Meta me tumhe problem yaad dilani padti hai. Dono kaam ke hain — bas business type alag hai.

Ek example lo: Laxmi Nagar ka ek dental clinic Google par root canal treatment Laxmi Nagar keyword par ad chalata hai — search karne wale ko turant appointment chahiye, to call rate bahut high hota hai. Wahi clinic Meta par smile makeover before-after reel chalata hai — dekhne wala aaj treatment nahi karayega, par do mahine baad jab dard hoga to wahi clinic yaad ayegi. Delhi me ROI-focused campaigns chalane wali Veltex Digital isi intent-audience match ko sabse pehle fix karti hai — kyunki galat platform par sahi ad bhi paisa jalata hai.

Dono platforms ke ad formats ek nazar me

  • Google Search Ads: text ads search results me — ready-to-buy customers, call aur WhatsApp leads ke liye best.
  • Google Performance Max aur YouTube: poore Google network par auto-placements — retargeting aur reach ke liye.
  • Meta Feed aur Reels Ads: photo, video aur carousel — visual products, offers aur brand building ke liye best.
  • Meta Lead Ads: app chhode bina form — saste leads, par quality filter karna padta hai.

2. Cost comparison 2026: CPC, CPM aur CPL Delhi benchmarks

Ab woh sawal jo sab poochte hain — kharcha kitna ayega? Neeche 2026 ke Delhi-NCR benchmarks hamare live campaigns par based hain. Yaad rakho, yeh ranges hain — exact cost tumhare niche, competition aur offer par depend karti hai.

  • Google Search CPC Delhi: local services ₹30–₹120 per click, clinics ₹60–₹200, real estate aur high-ticket ₹150–₹500. Competitive keywords me CPC sabse high hota hai.
  • Meta CPM Delhi: ₹150–₹400 per 1000 impressions — matlab reach Google se sasti hai. Click rate poori tarah creative par depend karta hai.
  • Google Search CPL: local services ₹250–₹700 per lead, clinics ₹500–₹1,200, real estate ₹800–₹2,500. Lead quality sabse high — caller khud phone karta hai.
  • Meta CPL: offers aur local services ₹120–₹400, clinics ₹300–₹700, ecommerce purchase ₹200–₹600 CPA. Leads saste padte hain, par follow-up system strong hona chahiye.

Matlab seedha hai — Meta se saste leads, Google se garam leads. ₹500 ka Google lead jo 20 percent convert ho, ₹150 ke Meta lead se sasta padta hai jo 3 percent convert ho. Isliye CPL ke sath cost-per-customer hamesha track karo. Poora kharcha samajhne ke liye hamara Hindi pricing guide dekho — usme retainer vs performance models ka breakdown hai. Platform-wise detail ke liye Google Ads Delhi ROI playbook aur Meta Ads Delhi ROI playbook dono English me ready hain.

3. Kaun se business ke liye kaun sa platform better

Yeh section sabse important hai — apna business type dhoondho aur seedha jawab pao:

  • Clinics (dental, physio, derma): Google Search pehle — patient dard me search karta hai. Meta retargeting ke liye — website visitors ko before-after results dikhao. Hamare clinic campaigns me 24 se 48 ghante me live calls isi combination se aati hain.
  • Real estate (builders, dealers): Google Search site-visit bookings ke liye, Meta Reels project walkthrough videos ke liye. High-ticket me dono chahiye — sirf ek platform se pipeline nahi bharegi.
  • Ecommerce aur D2C fashion: Meta pehle — Reels aur carousel se impulse purchase hota hai. Google Shopping aur branded search baad me, jab demand ban jaye.
  • Local services (salon, repair, packers): Google Search aur Map presence pehle — near me searches sabse garam hoti hain. Meta par festival offers chalao.
  • Coaching aur courses: YouTube aur Google Search admission season me, Meta Reels saal bhar demo-class leads ke liye. Dono ka mix sabse stable pipeline deta hai.
  • B2B aur agencies: Google Search high-intent keywords par, Meta sirf retargeting aur founder-led content ke liye. Cold B2B leads Meta par mehenge padte hain.

Golden rule yaad rakho — jitni urgent need, utna Google; jitna visual ya desire product, utna Meta. Dard, deadline aur demand Google par; fashion, food aur fun Meta par.

4. Delhi targeting setup: dono platforms par sahi geo-targeting

Delhi me sabse zyada paisa galat targeting me jalta hai. Karol Bagh ka salon Noida se clicks le raha hai — customer kabhi ayega nahi, par click ka paisa kat gaya. Dono platforms par targeting alag tareeke se set hoti hai:

  • Google me Presence option chuno: location setting me Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations select karo — Interest wale option se bahar ke clicks aate hain. Delhi ke serviceable areas (Rohini, Dwarka, Laxmi Nagar) alag ad groups me rakho.
  • Meta me radius aur pin-drop: address ke around 5–15 km radius, ya Delhi ke specific pin codes. Broad Delhi targeting chhote budget me paisa failata hai — pehle apne 10 km me jeeto.
  • Language layer: Delhi me Hinglish creatives dono platforms par English-only se better chalte hain — customer apni bhasha me baat karne wale par trust karta hai.
  • Google me negative locations: jahan service nahi dete (Delhi campaign me Gurgaon ya Faridabad), unhe exclude karo — warna budget padosi sheher kha jayega.
  • Call tracking lagao: kaun sa call Google se aaya, kaun sa Meta se — yeh pata nahi to budget andhere me hai. Har ad ka CPL likhit me track karo.

Hamari Delhi campaigns me Google Ads specialists aur Meta Ads experts geo-targeting ko weekly review karte hain — kyunki ek galat radius setting poore mahine ka ROAS bigaad deti hai.

5. Creatives aur landing pages: kahan kya chalta hai

Platform chun liya — ab ad me kya dikhaoge? Dono ki creative language bilkul alag hai:

  • Google Search me words jeetate hain: headline me keyword plus area plus offer — jaise Dentist Rohini | Aaj Hi Appointment | Painless Treatment. Call, location aur price extensions lagana mat bhoolo — CTR 15–20 percent badh jata hai.
  • Meta me pehle 3 second jeetate hain: Reels-style vertical video, real face, real shop — stock photos ko Delhi audience scroll karke nikal jati hai. Before-after, offer aur testimonial formats sabse zyada convert karte hain.
  • Landing page Google ke liye mandatory hai: ad wale keyword ka page par hona, click-to-call button, WhatsApp chat aur single-screen form — yahi Quality Score aur conversion badhata hai.
  • Meta ke liye speed aur simplicity: slow website Meta traffic ko maar deti hai — 3 second se zyada load to aadhe log wapas. Instant form ya WhatsApp redirect chhote business ke liye best hai.
  • Social proof dono jagah: Google reviews count ad me, Meta comments me real replies — Delhi customer padosi ki review par sabse zyada bharosa karta hai.

Ek sach yaad rakho — average creative plus great targeting, great creative plus wrong targeting se hamesha jeetata hai. Pehle sahi audience, phir creative polish. Isi order me hamare 50 se zyada brands ke campaigns scale hue hain — SEO se 1.2M se zyada impressions aur 300 percent growth wali discipline ads me bhi lagti hai.

6. Budget split formula, 30-day test plan aur badi galtiyan

Theory bahut ho gayi — ab seedha formula. Monthly ads budget ko aise baanto:

  • Budget ₹30,000 se kam: 100 percent ek platform par — upar section-3 se apna platform chuno. Split karoge to dono campaigns learning phase se bahar nahi niklengi.
  • Budget ₹30,000–₹80,000: 70 percent main platform (intent wala) plus 30 percent retargeting (dusra platform). Example: Google Search 70% plus Meta retargeting 30%.
  • Budget ₹80,000 se zyada: 50% Google plus 35% Meta prospecting plus 15% retargeting aur YouTube. Scale par teeno engines chahiye.

30-day test plan:

  • Week 1: tracking setup — conversion events, call tracking, WhatsApp click count. Bina tracking ke ek rupaya mat kharch karo.
  • Week 2: main platform launch — 2 campaigns, 3 ad variations. Budget roz mat chhedo.
  • Week 3: winner ad ko scale karo, loser band karo, retargeting audience banana shuru karo.
  • Week 4: retargeting launch plus CPL review. Jo CPL target se 30% upar hai, uska offer ya landing page badlo — pehle budget mat badhao.

Badi galtiyan jo Delhi businesses baar-baar karte hain: boost post button se ads chalana (Ads Manager ke bina targeting aur optimization milta hi nahi), pehle hafte me roz budget badalna, Meta lead forms par bina follow-up team ke leads lena, Google me broad-match keywords bina negative list ke, aur sabse badi — cost-per-customer track na karna. Leads ginna vanity hai, customers ginna business hai.
