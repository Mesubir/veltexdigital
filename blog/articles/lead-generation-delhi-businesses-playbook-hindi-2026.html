1. Delhi me lead generation alag game kyon hai

Delhi me har market me competition aasman par hai — ek hi road par 10 salon, 8 gym, 5 clinics aur 20 coaching centres. Aise me jo business roz naye enquiries ka system bana leta hai, wahi aage badhta hai. Referral aur walk-in kabhi predictable nahi hote. Delhi me ROI-focused growth chalane wali Veltex Digital isi predictable pipeline ko design karti hai — channel, budget aur funnel ka poora hisaab ke sath.

Samjho teen tarah ke customers hote hain. Pehle — jo abhi kharidna chahte hain, ye Google par search karte hain. Doosre — jo soch rahe hain, ye Instagram aur Facebook par content dekhte hain. Teesre — jo aas-paas dhoondh rahe hain, ye Google Maps par najdeeki dukaan dekhte hain. Lead generation ka matlab teeno jagah maujood rehna hai, taaki customer jahan bhi dhoondhe, tumhara naam mile.

  • Demand capture karo: Google Search Ads se un logon ko pakdo jo abhi service dhoondh rahe hain — ye sabse garam leads hote hain.
  • Demand create karo: Meta Ads aur reels se un logon tak pahuncho jo abhi soch bhi nahi rahe — ye pipeline bharta hai.
  • Local intent pakdo: Google Business Profile aur Maps se najdeeki search wale customers free me aate hain.
  • Trust compound karo: SEO blogs aur reviews se brand search badhta hai, aur brand search wale leads sabse saste me convert hote hain.

2. 2026 channel mix: Google, Meta, Maps aur SEO

Ek channel par tikna sabse bada risk hai. Algorithm badla, policy badli, ya competitor ne bid badha di — leads ruk jayenge. 2026 ka safe formula 50-30-20 mix hai: 50 percent budget demand capture par (Google Search + Maps optimization), 30 percent demand creation par (Meta Ads + reels), 20 percent long-term asset par (SEO content + reviews engine). Business type ke hisaab se mix badlo:

  • Clinic, salon, gym (walk-in): Maps + Google Ads 60 percent, Meta reels 30 percent, SEO 10 percent. Radius 5 se 10 km rakho.
  • Coaching aur courses (high enquiry volume): Meta leads 50 percent, Google Search 30 percent, YouTube + SEO 20 percent.
  • Real estate aur interiors (high ticket): Google Search 50 percent, Meta retargeting 30 percent, SEO aur reviews 20 percent. Yahan lead quality sab kuch hai.
  • B2B services aur agencies: SEO + content 50 percent, Google Search 30 percent, Meta retargeting 20 percent.

Maps aur local SEO ko halke me mat lo. Hamare Hindi Local SEO Map Pack guide me bataya gaya hai ki 3-pack me aane wali listing roz free calls la sakti hai — bina ek rupaya ad spend kiye.

Budget baantne ka seedha rule

Monthly marketing budget ka 60 percent proven channels par, 25 percent naye tests par (naya channel ya naya creative), 15 percent retargeting par lagao. Retargeting sabse sasta hota hai kyunki ye garam audience ko dobara chhoota hai — website visitors, video viewers aur purane enquiries.

3. Cost aur CPL: Delhi me ek lead kitne ka padta hai

2026 ke Delhi NCR benchmarks seedhe shabdon me. Dhyan rakho, ye ad spend hai (platform ko jaane wala paisa), agency fee iske upar alag lagti hai:

  • Mass segments (salon, gym, coaching, restaurant): 100 se 200 rupaye per lead — Meta par sasta, Google par thoda mehnga lekin quality behtar.
  • Mid ticket (clinic, interiors, courses): 200 se 350 rupaye per lead — dono platforms ka mix chalao.
  • High ticket (real estate, premium clinic, B2B): 400 se 800 rupaye per lead — yahan sasta lead matlab junk lead.
  • Google Maps aur organic calls: lagbhag zero marginal cost — lekin profile aur reviews me mehnat lagti hai.

Ek kadvi sachchai — sasta lead mehnga padta hai jab wo kharidta nahi. 80 rupaye wale 100 timepass leads se behtar hain 300 rupaye wale 25 genuine buyers. Hamesha cost-per-qualified-lead aur cost-per-sale dekho, sirf CPL nahi. Pura kharcha samajhne ke liye hamara Hindi agency pricing guide dekho — koi hidden charge nahi.

4. Funnel jo convert kare: landing page, WhatsApp aur 5-minute rule

Leads aana aadhi jeet hai — unhe customer banana asli khel hai. Delhi ka customer wait nahi karta. Jo pehle jawab deta hai, use hi order milta hai. Funnel aisa banao:

  • Ek page, ek offer, ek button: landing page par menu, gallery aur lambi kahani mat daalo. Sirf offer, trust proof aur bada WhatsApp ya call button.
  • Click-to-WhatsApp ads: form bharne ke jhanjhat ke bina chat khul jati hai. Service businesses ke liye ye sabse tez convert karta hai.
  • 5-minute reply rule: enquiry ka jawab 5 minute me do — 30 minute ke baad conversion rate aadha ho jata hai. Auto-greeting aur quick replies lagao.
  • Rate card ready rakho: price, photos, slot timing — customer poochhe usse pehle bhejo. Mystery pricing Delhi me nahi chalti.
  • Follow-up sequence: Day 1 reminder, Day 3 testimonial aur reviews, Day 7 limited-slot offer. 80 percent sales follow-up me hoti hai, pehle message me nahi.

Landing page slow hua to ad ka paisa paani me. 3 second se zyada load time matlab aadhe clicks waste. Hamari Delhi lead generation service me landing page, WhatsApp setup aur follow-up script — teeno ek system me milte hain.

5. Lead quality, scoring aur CRM: junk leads se chutkara

Har business ki shikayat — leads to aate hain, par timepass nikalte hain. Iska ilaj filter hai, zyada budget nahi:

  • Form me 2 se 3 qualifying sawal: budget range, location, timeline. Ye teeno jawab de de, wahi genuine hai. Fake entries yahin ruk jayengi.
  • Lead scoring: har lead ko A-B-C grade do. A matlab abhi kharidega — turant call karo. C matlab sirf research — use WhatsApp broadcast me rakho.
  • CRM me sab likho: diary aur memory par business mat chalao. Kaun sa lead kahan se aaya, kis stage par hai — CRM me dikhna chahiye.
  • Source-wise ROI nikalo: har mahine dekho — Google se kitni sales, Meta se kitni, Maps se kitni. Jo channel sale nahi la raha, uska budget kaato.

Sale ka hisaab seedha rakho

Simple maths — mahine ka ad spend / usse aayi confirmed sales = cost per sale. Agar ek sale se 5000 rupaye ka margin hai aur cost per sale 1200 hai, to system profitable hai — ab scale karo. Veltex Digital ke transparent packages yahan dekho — report me spend, leads aur revenue teeno hote hain.

6. Badi galtiyan aur 90-day predictable pipeline plan

Delhi ke business accounts audit karne par yahi 6 galtiyan baar-baar milti hain:

  • Sirf ek channel par nirbhar: ek din ad account band, agle din leads zero. Hamesha kam se kam 2 paid + 1 organic channel chalao.
  • Bina landing page ke ads: seedha homepage ya profile par traffic bhejna paise jalana hai.
  • Slow follow-up: ghanto baad reply dene wali team ke liye koi bhi CPL mehnga hai.
  • Junk leads par budget: qualifying sawal lagaye bina saste leads laoge to sales team ka time waste hoga.
  • No tracking: kaun sa lead kahan se aaya, pata nahi — to budget decision andhere me hoga. UTM aur CRM pehle din se lagao.
  • Retargeting zero: 97 percent log pehli baar me nahi kharidte. Dobara yaad dilana sabse sasta marketing hai.

Ab 90 din ka seedha plan:

  • Day 1 se 30: Google Business Profile fix karo, reviews mango. Ek landing page banao. Google + Meta par chhota budget test karo (500 se 800 rupaye roz). 5-minute reply system lagao.
  • Day 31 se 60: winner channel pehchano, loser band karo. Budget 30 se 50 percent badhao. Retargeting live karo. CRM me scoring shuru karo.
  • Day 61 se 90: SEO ke 4 se 6 blogs publish karo, lookalike audience banao, WhatsApp broadcast list tayaar karo. Source-wise ROI nikalo aur agle quarter ka budget fix karo.
