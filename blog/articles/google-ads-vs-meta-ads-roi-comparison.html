Google Ads vs Meta Ads: Which Delivers Higher ROI for Your Business?

An exhaustive multi-industry breakdown evaluating cost per lead (CPL), search intent alignment, creative conversion funnels, and return on ad spend (ROAS) across E-commerce, B2B, Real Estate, and Local Services.

1. Intent Capture vs Audience Discovery Mechanics

Choosing where to allocate your performance marketing budget depends on how your target buyers discover and evaluate products or services.

A. Google Search Ad Intent Dynamics

Users searching on Google (e.g., "hire commercial interior designer in Gurgaon" or "best CRM software for logistics companies") express active purchase intent. Search campaigns deliver faster conversion turnaround with average lead conversion rates between 7% and 15%.

B. Meta Visual Audience Discovery

Meta advertising (Facebook & Instagram) captures visual discovery for D2C brands, fashion e-commerce, real estate developments, and consumer lifestyle services. Meta ads rely on compelling video angles, UGC (User-Generated Content), and interactive carousels.

4. Top 10 Google Search Questions & Expert Answers (FAQ)

1. Which is better for business growth, Google Ads or Meta Ads?

Google Ads is superior for capturing active search intent (when users search for immediate solutions), while Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) is ideal for visual discovery, brand awareness, and retargeting passive audiences.

2. What is the average Cost Per Lead (CPL) for Meta Ads in India?

Average Meta Ads CPL in India ranges from ₹120 to ₹450 for B2C, real estate, and local lead forms, and ₹250 to ₹900 for B2B services, making social media ads cost-effective for top-of-funnel reach.

3. What is the average Cost Per Click (CPC) for Google Ads in India?

Google Ads CPC in India varies by industry competitiveness, averaging ₹15 to ₹55 per click for general services, and ₹80 to ₹350+ per click for high-ticket industries like real estate, finance, legal, and software.

4. How much minimum budget is needed to start running Google Search Ads in India?

A starting testing budget of ₹15,000 to ₹40,000 per month allows small businesses in India to gather click data, test keyword match types, and optimize high-converting landing pages.

5. Should E-commerce businesses prioritize Google Shopping Ads or Meta Catalog Ads?

E-commerce brands achieve maximum ROAS by using Meta Catalog Ads for visual impulse discovery and Google Shopping Ads to capture high-intent buyers searching for specific product models.

6. What is the difference between Google Search Ads and Display Ads?

Google Search Ads appear on SERPs when users search specific keywords. Display Ads are visual banner ads shown across Google partner websites and apps primarily for remarketing and brand awareness.

7. How does Meta retargeting pixel increase conversion rates?

The Meta Pixel tracks website visitors and serves tailored follow-up ads (like customer reviews, special offers, or product reminders) to users on Instagram and Facebook, recovering bounced traffic.

8. What is ROAS (Return On Ad Spend) and how do you calculate it?

ROAS is calculated by dividing total revenue generated from ad campaigns by total ad spend. For example, generating ₹4,000,000 in sales from ₹1,000,000 ad spend equals a 4.0x ROAS.

9. Why are my Meta Ads getting leads but zero sales conversions?

Low conversion from Meta leads usually stems from frictionless auto-filled lead forms, slow sales follow-up response times, or weak offer messaging. Implementing qualifying custom questions improves lead quality.

10. Why hire a PPC agency for ad campaign management instead of DIY ads?

Partnering with an agency like Veltex Digital prevents wasted ad spend through advanced negative keyword lists, Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO), custom A/B landing page creation, and continuous bid automation.
