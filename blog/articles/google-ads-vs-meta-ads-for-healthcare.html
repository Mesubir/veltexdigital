Google Ads vs Meta Ads for Healthcare: Which Offers Higher ROI in India?

An exhaustive performance marketing breakdown evaluating cost per lead (CPL), search intent alignment, creative conversion funnels, and customer lifetime value across Indian healthcare sectors.

1. Intent Capture vs Audience Discovery Mechanics

Allocating performance advertising budgets between search intent platforms and social discovery networks dictates patient acquisition efficiency for specialized medical practices.

A. Google Search Ad Intent Dynamics

Users performing active searches (e.g., "urgent root canal dentist near me" or "IVF consultation appointment cost") possess immediate conversion intent. Click-through conversion rates for search campaigns average 8% to 14%, resulting in faster booking turnaround.

B. Meta Visual Audience Discovery

Meta advertising (Facebook & Instagram) captures passive discovery for elective treatments such as hair transplants, smile design, or cosmetic surgery. Success requires visual story formats, patient video testimonials, and educational carousel ads.
