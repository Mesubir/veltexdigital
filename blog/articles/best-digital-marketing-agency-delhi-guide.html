2. The 7-Point Checklist Before You Hire

Use this checklist to filter Delhi agencies in one meeting:

  • Proven case studies with numbers: "300% growth" with screenshots beats "we helped many clients". Ask for CPL, ROAS, and timelines.
  • Transparent reporting: Real-time dashboard access, not monthly PDFs. You should see every rupee.
  • Flexible retainers: No rigid lock-ins. We operate on flexible monthly retainers — performance earns the next month.
  • Google certification: Our team holds Google certifications including Fundamentals of Digital Marketing, awarded by Google.
  • Niche experience: A generic agency burns clinic budgets fast. We run lead generation for healthcare — Vrinda Fertility and Dr Anushka Madan — plus 50+ brands across D2C and local services.
  • Full-funnel capability: SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, landing pages, and CRM follow-up under one roof. Leads without follow-up are donations.
  • Local Delhi understanding: Dwarka behaves nothing like South Ex. Your agency should know NCR micro-markets, Hindi-Hinglish audiences, and Map Pack dynamics.

3. Delhi-Specific Factors Most Agencies Miss

Delhi NCR is India's most competitive local SERP. Three things decide winners here. First, Google Maps dominance — for clinics, salons, and institutes, the Map Pack drives more calls than organic listings. Second, Hinglish content — a large share of Delhi searches and ad clicks respond better to Hindi-mixed messaging. Third, speed-to-lead — the first business to call back within 5 minutes wins the patient or admission in most categories we track.

4. Red Flags That Should Make You Walk Away

  • Guaranteed #1 rankings: Nobody can guarantee Google positions. We promise process and pilots, not positions.
  • No ad account access: If the agency won't add you to the ad account, your money is a black box.
  • One-size packages: A ₹15,000 fixed package for a dental chain and a coaching institute is astrology, not marketing.
  • No negative keywords or lead filters: Junk inquiries mean the agency optimizes for clicks, not customers.
