1. Patient ab Google par clinic choose karta hai — behaviour samjho

Delhi NCR me sehat se juda har faisla ab phone se shuru hota hai. Danton me dard ho, skin problem ho ya bacche ke liye pediatrician dhundhna ho — patient pehle Google par best dentist near me, skin specialist Rohini, IVF centre Delhi ya physiotherapist Dwarka search karta hai. Jo clinic top results aur map me dikhti hai, wahi appointment pati hai. Referral ab bhi kaam karta hai, lekin referral milne ke baad bhi patient Google par tumhara naam, rating aur reviews zaroor check karta hai.

Clinic marketing aur normal business marketing me ek bada fark hai — yahan customer koi product nahi, bharosa kharid raha hai. Google bhi health content ko YMYL (Your Money Your Life) category me rakhta hai, matlab clinic websites par quality aur trust ke rules aur strict hain. Wahi clinic rank karegi jiske paas real doctor credentials, genuine reviews aur sahi medical information ho. Delhi me ROI-focused campaigns chalane wali Veltex Digital isi trust ko Google ranking aur confirmed appointments me badalti hai.

Patient ka decision journey samjho:

  • Search: symptom ya treatment + area likh kar search — jaise braces cost Laxmi Nagar.
  • Compare: top 3-4 clinics ki rating, review count, photos aur fees compare karta hai.
  • Verify: doctor ka naam, qualification aur clinic ki website check karta hai. Isi stage ka detail hamare clinics ke liye English SEO guide me bhi mil jayega.
  • Book: call, WhatsApp ya online form se appointment — jo sabse easy lage.

Is pure journey me Google hi referee hai. Ranking me ho to patient tumhara, nahi ho to competitor ka — itna simple hai.

2. Google Business Profile: clinic ki sabse important listing

Google Business Profile (GBP) Google ki free listing hai — clinic ke liye yeh website se bhi zyada calls lati hai, kyunki map results me call button seedha laga hota hai. 2026 me Google ne health listings ka verification aur strict kar diya hai, isliye har field sahi bharna pehle se zyada zaroori ho gaya hai.

  • Category bilkul sahi chuno: Dental Clinic, Skin Care Clinic, Physiotherapist ya IVF centre — generic Clinic category lene se specific treatment searches me rank nahi milega.
  • Doctor profiles alag banao: clinic ke sath senior doctors ke practitioner listings bhi verify karwao — patient doctor ke naam par bhi search karta hai.
  • Hours aur appointment link: OPD timing, Sunday availability aur online booking link hamesha updated rakho. Galat timing par band clinic mili to 1-star review pakka hai.
  • Real photos har hafte: reception, treatment rooms, equipment aur team ki photos dalo. Stock photos se bacho — patient asli clinic dekhna chahta hai.
  • Services me treatments likho: har treatment ke sath 2-3 line simple bhasha me description — root canal, braces, laser hair removal, jaise patient bolta hai waise.
  • Posts aur updates: hafte me ek post — naya equipment, health camp, festive offer ya doctor introduction. Active profile Google ko signal deta hai ki clinic zinda hai.
  • Q and A ready rakho: fees, appointment process, parking, report timing — common sawal khud pooch kar jawab do.

Ek common galti — clinic ka naam board par kuch aur, Practo par kuch aur aur Google par kuch aur. NAP (Name, Address, Phone) har platform par character-by-character same rakho, warna Google confuse hota hai aur ranking girti hai. Purani galat listings ko update ya remove karwao.

3. Treatment pages aur doctor E-E-A-T: website ka asli kaam

Clinic ki website ka kaam sirf sundar dikhna nahi — patient ke sawalon ka jawab dena hai. Sabse powerful strategy hai har treatment ke liye alag page banana. Ek hi Services page par 20 treatments ki list se Google kuch rank nahi karta. Root canal, braces, dental implants, acne treatment — har ek ka apna page, apni fees range, procedure steps, recovery time aur FAQs ke sath.

Har treatment page par yeh 5 cheezein zaroor hon:

  • Patient wali bhasha: mushkil medical English nahi — dard hoga kya, kitne visits lagenge, kharcha kitna aayega.
  • Before-after ya procedure photos: consent ke sath real cases — yeh conversion ka sabse bada hathiyar hai.
  • Fees transparency: exact range likho. Fees chhupane wali clinics par patient bharosa nahi karta.
  • Doctor ka naam aur link: yeh treatment kaun doctor karta hai, uske profile page ka link do.
  • Appointment CTA: har page par call aur WhatsApp button — patient ko sochne ka time mat do, book karne ka rasta do.

Doctor E-E-A-T: Google clinic websites me kya dekhta hai

Google health websites ko E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) par parkhta hai. Iska matlab practical bhasha me:

  • Har doctor ka detail profile page: qualification, registration number, saalon ka anubhav, specialization aur photo.
  • Content doctor ke naam se: blog ya treatment info par author ke roop me doctor ka naam — anonymous health advice Google ko pasand nahi.
  • Schema markup: MedicalClinic aur Physician schema lagao taaki Google tumhare clinic aur doctors ko sahi samjhe.
  • External mentions: Practo, Lybrate, JustDial aur medical directories par consistent profiles — yeh authority banate hain.

Hamari performance campaigns me SEO se 1.2M se zyada impressions aur 300 percent growth isi E-E-A-T wale kaam se aaya hai. Health me shortcut nahi chalta — real credentials hi ranking dilate hain.

4. Patient reviews: clinic ki online reputation hi OPD hai

Ek kadvi sachchai suno — 4.7 rating wali 200 reviews wali clinic, 5.0 rating wali 8 reviews wali clinic ko hara deti hai. Patient count dekhta hai, perfect score nahi. Aur Google bhi naye reviews ki speed ko ranking signal ki tarah dekhta hai. Reviews clinic ke liye utne hi zaroori hain jitne OPD me patient — iska poora patient-side system hamare patient acquisition guide me bhi samjhaya gaya hai.

  • Discharge par QR code: reception par Google review ka QR stand rakho. Treatment se khush patient usi 2 minute me review de deta hai — ghar jakar koi nahi deta.
  • WhatsApp follow-up: visit ke ek din baad short message — recovery poochho, sath me review link. Care bhi, review bhi.
  • Staff ki ek line: team ko sikhao — jab patient khud tareef kare, tabhi politely review ke liye kaho. Jabardasti mange reviews me jhalakti hai.
  • Har review ka jawab 24 se 48 ghante me: positive par thanks plus treatment ka naam naturally, negative par calm professional reply.
  • Privacy ka niyam: jawab me patient ka naam, bimari ya treatment ka zikr kabhi mat karo. Baat ko phone par resolve ke liye clinic ka number do.
  • Fake reviews kabhi mat kharido: achanak 40 five-star reviews Google turant pakad leta hai — listing suspend, aur wapas lana mahino ka kaam. Hamare Hindi pricing guide ke hisab se genuine review system lagana shortcut se hamesha sasta padta hai.

Reviews sirf rating nahi, free ka feedback bhi hain. Patients baar-baar waiting time, staff behaviour ya billing confusion likh rahe hain? Wahi tumhara agla improvement plan hai — aur wahi cheez theek karke review me batana, naye patients ka bharosa jeet-ta hai.

5. Area-wise pages aur Hindi content: Rohini se Noida tak

Delhi NCR me patient hamesha apne area me clinic dhundhta hai — koi Dwarka se Laxmi Nagar nahi jayega. Isliye ek generic Delhi page se kaam nahi chalega. Har area ke liye alag page banao — jaise Dental Clinic in Rohini, Skin Specialist in Dwarka, Physiotherapist in Noida. Har page par unique content ho: us area ke landmarks, metro connectivity, map embed, area-specific FAQs aur us area ke patient reviews.

Copy-paste karke sirf area ka naam badalne wale pages Google pakad leta hai — har page mehnat mangta hai. Har area page par us area ki common problems bhi likho — jaise paani ki wajah se skin issues us area ke patients kya poochhte hain, wahi likho.

Hindi aur Hinglish content: jo patient bolta hai, wahi likho

Delhi ka patient daanton ka doctor, chamdi ke doctor, kamar dard ka ilaj bhi search karta hai — sirf dentist ya dermatologist nahi. Apne content me dono bhasha mix karo: heading me English treatment naam, body me simple Hindi explanation. Symptom-based blogs likho — daanton me thand-garam lagna, chehre par daag-dhabbe, ghutne ka dard. Symptom search karne wala patient treatment page tak khud pahunchta hai.

Iske upar local backlinks lo — Delhi health blogs, RWA groups, school health camps aur partner pharmacies se mentions. Ek real local website ka link 100 spam directory links se behtar hai. Budget aur package planning ke liye Veltex Digital ke transparent packages yahan dekho.

6. SEO + Google Ads combo, 30-day plan aur badi galtiyan

SEO me 3 se 6 mahine lagte hain — tab tak OPD khali thodi rakhenge. Isliye smart clinics SEO plus Google Ads sath chalati hain. Ads se aaj se calls, SEO se mahino baad free calls. Ads ka data (kaunsi search se call aayi) SEO content plan me use karo — dono ek-dusre ko tez karte hain. High-value treatments (IVF, implants, transplant) me Ads ka CPL track karo — yeh pata hona chahiye ki ek patient kitne me pada.

Theory bahut ho gayi — ab 30 din ka seedha plan:

  • Week 1: GBP ka full audit — category, hours, services, photos. NAP fix karke Practo, JustDial aur Lybrate profiles update karo.
  • Week 2: review system live karo — QR stand, WhatsApp template, staff training. Pehle 20 genuine reviews ka target rakho.
  • Week 3: top 2 treatments ke detail pages publish karo plus pehla area page. Q and A me 5 common sawal khud add karo.
  • Week 4: call tracking lagao, map position note karo aur Google Ads ka chhota pilot shuru karo. Jo kaam kar raha hai, use double karo.

Badi galtiyan jo clinics baar-baar karti hain: profile me galat category, doctor ke naam par keyword thonsna, ek hi clinic ke duplicate map profiles, fees chhupana, patient privacy tod kar review jawab me bimari likhna, aur sabse badi — tracking na lagana. Call kahan se aayi, yeh pata nahi to marketing budget andhere me phenkna hai.
