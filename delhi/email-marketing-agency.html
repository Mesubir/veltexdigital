Our Email Revenue Architecture

How We Turn Your List Into a Revenue Channel

Most agencies treat email as a newsletter chore — one generic blast a week to the full list, landing in promotions, unsubscribed within a month. We treat it as a revenue channel with three load-bearing pillars. Every engagement starts with a forensic audit of your current account — list health, flow coverage, deliverability authentication, template quality and revenue attribution — and only then do we write the first subject line. Remove any one pillar and the channel collapses back into spam-folder noise.

Flows That Sell While You Sleep

Automated flows are the highest-ROI emails you will ever send, and most Delhi brands run three or fewer. We deploy a minimum 12-flow foundation: multi-step welcome series with first-order incentives, browse and cart abandonment sequences with staged urgency, post-purchase cross-sell and review-request flows, replenishment reminders for consumables, winback series for lapsed buyers, and sunset flows that stop mailing the chronically unengaged before they torch your sender reputation. Each flow is segmented by purchase history, average order value and engagement recency, with conditional splits for COD versus prepaid buyers — a distinction that decides profitability in Delhi NCR ecommerce. Typical result: flows grow from under 5 percent to 55 to 65 percent of total email revenue within 120 days of launch.

Campaigns People Actually Open

Batch-and-blast is why your open rates sit at 12 percent. We send every campaign to segmented audiences — engaged-30-day, category buyers, high-AOV VIPs, clinic recall lists — with Hinglish and English copy variants matched to the segment. Delhi inboxes respond to cultural timing: festive drops for Diwali and Dhanteras, wedding-season edits from November to February, end-of-season sales timed to Lajpat Nagar and Sarojini footfall cycles, and payday-week pushes that consistently beat month-start sends. Every campaign ships with at least 8 subject-line and preview-text variants a month in structured A and B tests, and losers are retired within 48 hours. Our engaged-segment benchmark is 42 percent opens and 3.8 percent clicks — roughly triple the Indian retail average.

List Growth Plus Deliverability Engineering

A brilliant flow means nothing if Gmail routes it to spam. We grow your list with intent-matched capture — gamified popups with category-specific offers, style and skin quizzes, lead magnets for coaches and clinics, and checkout consent optimisation that typically lifts capture rates from 2 to 7 percent of sessions. In parallel we harden deliverability: SPF, DKIM and DMARC authentication on dedicated sending domains, monthly list hygiene that suppresses dead addresses, role accounts and spam traps, engagement-based sending cadence, and gradual warm-up for new domains. Dead weight is sunset aggressively, because mailing 50,000 cold contacts destroys the inbox placement your 8,000 buyers deserve. Inbox placement is monitored weekly and reported alongside revenue — never hidden.
