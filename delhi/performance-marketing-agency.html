Our Performance Operating System

How a Performance Marketing Agency in Delhi Should Actually Work

Most agencies optimise for the metrics that make their monthly report look good, impressions, clicks, even cheap leads that never buy. Our operating system flips that. Before we touch your ad account, we build a revenue model from your average order value, repeat-purchase rate, sales-team close rate and target CAC. Every campaign, creative and landing page is then judged against one question: did it move revenue per rupee spent? That discipline rests on three load-bearing pillars, and removing any one of them collapses ROI.

Full-Funnel Paid Media Sprints

Google Search captures buyers at the exact moment of intent, Meta and Instagram manufacture demand upstream, and YouTube plus retargeting close the undecided. We run structured testing sprints, a minimum of 15 creative and audience variants per account per month, kill losers within 72 hours on statistically valid spend, and reallocate budgets to proven winners. Day-parting around your sales hours, sector-level geo-fencing across Delhi NCR, and ruthless negative-keyword fortresses keep wasted spend near zero. Between month one and month four, our accounts typically see cost-per-acquisition fall 30 to 45 percent as learnings compound.

Landing Pages and CRO That Compound

Traffic is worthless if the page leaks. We build dedicated, mobile-first landing pages that load in under 2 seconds, carry one single call-to-action, and mirror the exact promise of the ad that sent the click. Trust signals such as Google reviews, before-after proof, clinic registrations, RERA numbers and EMI badges, plus sticky call and WhatsApp buttons, short OTP-verified forms and exit-intent offers, lift conversion rates from the Delhi average of 2 to 3 percent into our benchmark band of 8 to 14 percent. Every page is heat-mapped and session-recorded, and we ship CRO iterations fortnightly. Media buys traffic, CRO decides what that traffic is worth.

Revenue Tracking and CRM Feedback Loops

Ad platforms can only optimise for what you feed them. We implement server-side tracking, offline-conversion imports from your CRM, and sub-60-second lead routing into LeadSquared, Zoho, HubSpot or WhatsApp Business API. When a lead becomes a paying customer weeks later, that revenue signal flows back into Google and Meta, so bidding algorithms chase buyers instead of form-fillers. Combined with OTP verification, duplicate detection and intent scoring, your sales team calls genuine prospects, and your dashboards report revenue, CAC and payback period instead of vanity clicks.
