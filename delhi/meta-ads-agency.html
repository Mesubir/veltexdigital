Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Boosted Posts and Ads Manager?

Boosting a post is a basic feature optimized purely for engagement (likes/comments). As a professional Meta Ads Agency, we use the backend Meta Ads Manager to build complex, multi-stage funnels optimized directly for Purchases or Lead Generation, utilizing advanced tracking and Custom Audiences.

I've wasted money on Facebook ads before. Why will it work this time?

Most businesses fail at Meta Ads due to poor tracking, weak ad creatives, or targeting overly broad audiences. We fix your foundational tracking (Conversion API) first, then implement a rigorous A/B testing methodology to mathematically discover the creatives and audiences that yield the highest ROAS.

How much ad spend do I need to start?

While it varies by industry, we typically recommend a minimum ad spend of ₹30,000 to ₹50,000 per month for local lead generation in Delhi. For national E-commerce campaigns, higher budgets are required to properly exit the "learning phase" and allow the Meta algorithm to optimize efficiently.

Do you provide the ad creatives and copy?

Yes. Our service includes end-to-end campaign management. Our in-house team designs the static graphics, carousels, and writes the direct-response copywriting specifically tailored to convert your target demographic.
