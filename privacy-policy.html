Your Data, Protected by Law. Read Our Privacy Policy

This page explains in plain language what data our forms collect and exactly how we use it. We collect your details only to contact you back, understand your business, and provide marketing services that grow it — as required by India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Who We Are

Veltex Digital ("we", "us") operates veltexdigital.in, a digital marketing agency serving clinics and businesses across India. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India.

2. What Data We Collect

When you fill any form on our website (contact, audit request, newsletter, quote), we collect only what you type in: name, email address, phone number, business details, and your message. We also receive basic technical data (device, browser, pages visited) through analytics cookies to improve site performance.

3. Why We Collect It (Purpose)

Your form data is used strictly to reach out to you, connect with your business, understand your requirements, and provide marketing services that grow your business — including follow-up calls, WhatsApp messages, emails, quotes, audits, and service delivery. We do not use your data for any unrelated purpose, and we never sell your personal data to anyone.

4. Consent

Submitting a form on our website counts as your free, specific, and informed consent to be contacted for the purposes above, as required by the DPDP Act. You may withdraw consent at any time by emailing support@veltexdigital.in — we will stop marketing outreach and delete your data on request, subject to legal record-keeping duties.

5. How Forms Deliver Data (Third Parties)

Our contact forms are delivered through FormSubmit, which relays your submission to our team email. Analytics run through Google Tag Manager / Google Analytics. These processors handle data only on our instructions. FormSubmit servers may sit outside India — by submitting a form you acknowledge this cross-border transfer, which occurs solely to deliver your enquiry to us.

6. Data Retention

We keep enquiry data only as long as needed for follow-up and service delivery, and delete or anonymize it when no longer required, unless the law requires longer storage (e.g., invoices and tax records).

7. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to share, correct, update, or erase your personal data, and to stop contacting you. You may also nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf. Email support@veltexdigital.in from your registered email ID and we respond within 30 days.

8. Grievance Redressal

For any privacy complaint, contact our Grievance Officer at support@veltexdigital.in, Veltex Digital, Azadpur, Delhi – 110033. We acknowledge grievances within 48 hours and resolve them within 30 days. Unresolved concerns may be raised before the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Security

Our site runs over HTTPS, access to enquiry inboxes is restricted to authorized team members, and we review access periodically. No internet transmission is 100% secure, but we apply reasonable safeguards required by law, and we notify affected users and the Board in case of a personal data breach.

10. Children's Data

Our services are directed at businesses, not children. We do not knowingly collect data of individuals under 18; if you believe a child shared data with us, contact us and we will delete it promptly after guardian verification.

11. Cookies

We use basic analytics cookies to understand aggregate site usage. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the site remains usable, though some interactive features may degrade.

12. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when our practices or the law change. The "Last updated" date below always shows the current version. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the revised policy.
